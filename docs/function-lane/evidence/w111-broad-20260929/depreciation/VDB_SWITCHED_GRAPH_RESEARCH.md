# Switched VDB arithmetic research

This records the failed candidates, distinguishing probes and frozen validations
that led to the current explicit production graph. The final production replay
matches 113,329 switched numeric observations, including 20,000 fresh research
publication observations and a separate 8,000-row production dispatch cohort.
Fresh typed dispatch matches 522/522, with execution admitted on both sides.
Status: `scope_completeness=scope_partial`, `target_completeness=target_partial`,
`integration_completeness=partial`. Open lanes: large-period progress, shared
locale-sensitive coercion, receiving-dependency integration and alternate axes.
The history below preserves superseded hypotheses as discovery evidence.

The retained interval traces rule out a cumulative-total difference formula.
For cost 1000, salvage 100, life 1.5, and factor 1, the observed intervals
`[0,1.25]` and `[1.25,1.5]` sum to 930, while `[0,1.5]` returns 900.
`switched-vdb-counterexamples.json` preserves the exact witness identities and
bits. These observations instead support applying the fractional part of the
start period first, before advancing its whole years. A partial first year thus
changes the basis on which subsequent whole years decline.

The initial research graph also distinguishes a strict straight-line comparison
from equality, retains salvage clipping before later-year straight-line choice,
and accumulates the remaining straight-line segment as one product rather than
repeatedly adding an identical annual amount. It forms the shifted interval end
before subtracting whole start years. A negative initial basis is not replaced
by an assumed exact zero: the first book subtraction can leave a small residual
which Excel publishes in the following interval.

This graph matches 4,946 of 4,996 captured bounded switched-VDB discovery rows.
The remaining 50 comparisons differ by at most four ULP; none has been waived.
The positive-basis subset is 3,832 of 3,882. The input files come from the primary
capture, original discriminator, interval refinement, and 2,473-row trace
packet. These results are selection evidence from observed answers, not heldout
validation. The research driver checks an explicit interval bound below 1000;
the dangerous large-index packet is never fed to it.

`refinement3-vdb-switched.json` contains 770 bounded traces selected from those
50 residuals. It compares zero, fractional, and original starts and short end

The subsequent 2,256 longer transition traces match 1,983 times and retain 273

The separately frozen research graph was then evaluated against 5,000 fresh

Subsequent single-operation discrimination isolates another branch: when the
advance phase has already entered straight-line mode, the requested interval
uses the original `end-start` subtraction. Shifting both endpoints remains
observable when straight-line mode begins later. This research revision matches
4,990/5,000, resolving eight residuals without regressing the earlier 4,996,
770 or 2,256 packets (4,946, 770 and 1,983 exact respectively). The remaining
ten independent-origin residuals span one through seven ULP. They are discovery
for this revision, not a fresh independent success. Applying `end-start`
unconditionally matches only 4,981/5,000 and 1,773/2,256, so that alternative is
explicitly rejected by the retained observations.

`switched-research-endpoint-freeze.json` preserves source and selector
12884904146 before a fresh 324-case endpoint/phase discriminator. It changes

The 324 live outcomes distinguish the initial clipping research (244 exact),
the frozen inherited-straight-line revision (295 exact), and an extension for
straight-line selection on the first requested step (312 exact). Local exact
rational RN64/store traces then identify the general arithmetic rule behind
these branches: form the absolute left period `start+year`, store it, then
subtract it from `end`. Straight-line mode takes that remaining interval;
declining mode takes its minimum with one. This rule subsumes both special
straight-line endpoint branches and matches all 5,000 and all 324 observations.
It improves earlier bounded discovery to 4,986/4,996 and transition traces to
2,096/2,256, retaining 770/770 short traces. The ten original residual tuples
and 160 derived transition traces still differ by one or two ULP.
